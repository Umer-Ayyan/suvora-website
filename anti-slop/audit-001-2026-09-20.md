# Anti Slop Audit Report 001

- **Date:** 2026-09-20
- **Project:** Suvora Tech Website (`suvora-website`)
- **Audit Mode:** Mode 2 (After / Post-build Audit)
- **Status:** Awaiting User Approval on Finding Numbers

---

## Numbered Findings List

### Tier 1: Hard Gate (Priority: HIGH)

1. **[R-02] Copywriting (Em Dash Forbidden)**: Em dashes (`—`) are present in UI copy across `App.tsx` (lines 28, 58, 218, 223, 226, 227), `constants.tsx` (line 85), `pages/Contact.tsx` (lines 23, 43), and `pages/Services.tsx` (line 23). Must be replaced with commas, periods, colons, or parentheses.
2. **[R-17] Data & Numbers (Unverified & Inconsistent Statistics)**: Inconsistent and unverified statistics across pages: `App.tsx` claims "15+ Projects Delivered, 2 Global Hubs" while `About.tsx` claims "10+ Projects, 5+ International Clients, 24/7 Support".
3. **[R-17 & R-36] Data & Numbers (Fabricated Case Study Metrics)**: Unverified performance claims in `constants.tsx` and `CaseStudies.tsx` ("1M+ transactions/sec", "+40% User Retention", "+240% User Growth").
4. **[R-23 & R-38] Visual Assets (Stock / Cartoon Team Portraits)**: `constants.tsx` (lines 118, 125) uses Freepik stock illustrations (a semi-robot graphic for CEO and a cartoon vector for HR Manager) as team photos instead of real photos or honest initial-based avatars.
5. **[R-24 & R-26] Navigation & Interactive Elements (Dead Links)**:
   - `App.tsx` (line 108): `<a href="#">View Case Studies</a>` leads nowhere.
   - `components/Layout.tsx` (line 101): Footer logo links to `href="#"`.
   - `components/Layout.tsx` (lines 166-167): "Privacy Policy" and "Terms of Service" in the footer are static `<span>` elements with no link destination.
6. **[R-26] Interactive Elements (Dead Action Buttons)**:
   - `App.tsx` (line 147): `<button>Read Case Study</button>` has no `onClick` handler or route.
   - `pages/CaseStudies.tsx` (lines 93, 144): `<button>View Project</button>` buttons are completely non-functional.
7. **[R-27] UI States (Missing Error State Feedback)**: `components/ContactForm.tsx` handles submission and success, but on error (`status === 'error'`), it fails to display an error alert/message to the user.
8. **[R-32] Accessibility & Best Practices (Social Links Missing Rel)**: Social links in `components/Layout.tsx` (WhatsApp, Instagram, LinkedIn) use `target="_blank"` without `rel="noopener noreferrer"`.
9. **[R-38] Real Content or Honest Placeholder (Fictional Case Studies)**: `constants.tsx` contains fictional placeholder clients ("NovaFinance", "ShopifyPlus Brand") with generic stock images alongside the genuine product ("FlowERP").

---

### Tier 2: Purpose-Gate (Priority: MEDIUM)

10. **[R-01] Color & Gradients (Default AI Neon Glows)**: Stacked violet/cyan radial background blur orbs (`bg-suvora-primary/20 rounded-full blur-3xl`) used across `HeroSection.tsx`, `App.tsx`, and `ContactForm.tsx` without functional hierarchy.
11. **[R-08] Button Arrows (Repetitive Decorative Arrows)**: Arrows (`→`, `ArrowRight`, `&rarr;`) added by default to almost every CTA (`App.tsx`, `HeroSection.tsx`, `Careers.tsx`, `Services.tsx`).
12. **[R-09] Badges (Overused Capsule Badges)**: Generic pill badges (`px-3 py-1 rounded-full bg-violet-500/10 border`) used repeatedly across `App.tsx` and `Careers.tsx` without functional status meaning.
13. **[R-10 & R-13] Glassmorphism & Glow (Trend-Stacking)**: Simultaneous backdrop blur (`backdrop-blur-md`) and glowing box shadows (`hover:shadow-suvora-primary/10`, `shadow-[0_0_20px_rgba(...)]`) on nav, cards, and buttons.
14. **[R-14] Feature Cards (Monotonous Card Grids)**: `Services.tsx` and `App.tsx` display 4 to 6 identical cards with identical dimensions, icon containers, and padding with zero compositional variation.

---

### Tier 3: Quality Locks (Priority: LOW)

15. **[R-05] Layout & Page Structure (Template Section Rhythm)**: `App.tsx` follows the classic predictable AI SaaS rhythm (Hero -> Product highlight -> 4-card service grid -> 2-card portfolio -> tech marquee -> 3-member team -> SEO grid -> Contact form).
16. **[R-15] Generic CTAs**:
    - `Layout.tsx` (line 60): Uses generic "Get Started".
    - `Services.tsx` (lines 75, 100): Uses repetitive "Learn More →" on all cards pointing to the generic contact page.
17. **[R-16] Copywriting Buzzwords**: Uses generic agency clichés ("bleeding edge of web technology", "code is the clay of the 21st century", "ready to build the impossible", "modern stacks").
18. **[R-21] Dark Mode Default**: The entire site is locked to dark mode with no light mode toggle option.
