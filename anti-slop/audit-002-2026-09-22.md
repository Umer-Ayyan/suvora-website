## Numbered Findings List

### Tier 1: Hard Gate (Priority: HIGH — Absolute Rules)

1. **[R-17 & R-36] Data & Numbers (Unverified 99.9% Uptime Stat)**:
   `components/HeroCarousel.tsx` (line 73) claims *"99.9% uptime architectures"*. R-17 strictly bans statistics without a verified benchmark or monitoring SLA, specifically listing "99.9% Uptime" as an AI tell.
2. **[R-08 & R-15] CTAs & Decorative Arrows (Cookie Banner & Careers)**:
   - `components/CookieBanner.tsx` (line 37): Uses generic template CTA *"Learn More"*. Must be specific (e.g., *"Cookie Policy"*).
   - `pages/Careers.tsx` (line 108): CTA button contains a trailing decorative arrow (`Apply Now &rarr;`). R-08 bans trailing arrows as default decoration.
3. **[R-04] Icons (Generic Sparkles Icon in Section Badge)**:
   `components/HeroCarousel.tsx` (lines 12, 178): Uses `<Sparkles className="w-3.5 h-3.5" />` inside the section badge. R-04 explicitly forbids Sparkle/Star/Magic icons as AI default decorative filler.
4. **[R-04] Icons (Irrelevant Rocket Icon for Business Availability)**:
   `pages/Contact.tsx` (line 103): Uses a `<Rocket />` icon to represent "Availability" (*"Open for freelance, startup, and enterprise projects"*). A calendar or status indicator is relevant to scheduling/availability.

### Tier 2: Purpose-Gate (Priority: MEDIUM — Technique Allowed with Purpose)

5. **[R-08] Button Arrows (Repetitive Arrows across Carousel Cards)**:
   `components/HeroCarousel.tsx` (line 270): Every single capability card renders `<ArrowRight className="w-4 h-4" />`. R-08 states arrows must serve a clear directional purpose rather than being stamped onto every card CTA.
6. **[R-16] Copywriting Buzzwords ("delight eyes" & "Native-feel")**:
   - `constants.tsx` (line 71): *"Futuristic, accessible interfaces that guide user behavior and delight eyes."* — "delight eyes" is empty AI fluff.
   - `constants.tsx` (line 65): *"Native-feel cross-platform apps..."* — vague AI marketing terminology.
7. **[R-16] Copywriting Clichés in Capability Cards**:
   `components/HeroCarousel.tsx`:
   - Line 42: *"Intelligent AI Agents & Pipelines"* — redundant buzzword phrasing.
   - Line 83: *"combine clean aesthetics, seamless user journeys, and robust accessibility standards"* — "seamless" and "robust" are flagged in `antislop-copywriting` Empty AI Vocabulary.
8. **[R-16] Redundant Hero Subtitle Tagline**:
   `components/HeroSection.tsx` (line 72): Subtitle begins with `{SUVORA_CONFIG.tagline}` (*"We build the future, in code."*), immediately echoing the large H1 headline directly above it (*"We build the future, in code."*).

### Tier 3: Quality Locks (Priority: LOW — Consistency & Hygiene)

   - `pages/Careers.tsx` (line 23): `// Fetch dynamic jobs from CRM`.

    `pages/Careers.tsx` (line 92): When no jobs are returned, the UI renders Roman Urdu: *"Filhal koi open position mojood nahi hai. Dobara check karein!"* on an otherwise English corporate website.
11. **[R-16] SEO Buzzwords in Regional Grid**:
    `App.tsx` (line 226): Uses generic *"AI-powered automation, chatbots, intelligent workflows..."*. Ground to concrete capabilities (LLMs, automations, custom data pipelines).
